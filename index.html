Beyond Tech: Music

Before pivoting to cybersecurity, I spent seven years as a classical pianist and piano teacher in Baja California, Mexico. I hold a Bachelor's of Music from the Autonomous University of Baja California with a concentration in Piano Performance.

My music career included private instruction, collaborative chamber performances, and teaching at the State Center for the Arts (CEART) and the Baja California Institute of Culture (ICBC), where I mentored students awarded the Artistic Talents scholarship.

In 2020, I was awarded FONDARTE by the Baja California Institute of Culture for a collaboration project with the Classical Femmes ensemble — a grant supporting independent artistic and cultural activities.

This background gives me a distinctive edge: the discipline of performance, the patience of teaching, and the creativity of artistic collaboration — all of which I bring to my technical work every day.
